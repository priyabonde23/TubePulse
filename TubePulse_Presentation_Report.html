🎯 Viva & Interview Q&A Cheatsheet

Answers to tough questions asked by examiners & interviewers

Q1: How does your sentiment analysis handle Hinglish and Indian Slang?

Ans: We engineered a custom weighted contextual lexicon in lib/nlpEngine.ts supporting Indian terms like 'paisa wasool', 'bawaal', 'bakwas', alongside Gen-Z slang like 'fire', 'goat', 'mid' with negation handling and emoji scoring.

Q2: What is ABSA (Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis) and why is it superior?

Ans: Standard sentiment analysis only gives a single overall score. ABSA divides the video into 5 specific dimensions (Audio, Visuals, Explanation, Acting, Value) so creators know the exact component driving praise or friction.

Q3: How does the AI Voice Briefing work without paid APIs?

Ans: We combine the native browser Web Speech Synthesis API with a custom synthesis engine (lib/voiceBriefingEngine.ts) to deliver zero-latency, natural, and free audio playback.

Q4: Is this architecture production-ready and scalable?

Ans: Yes. The serverless Next.js API layer deployed on Vercel Global Edge CDN auto-scales independently across global regions with instant caching and high concurrency.
