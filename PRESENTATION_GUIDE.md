## ❓ 2. Problem Statement (What problem are we solving?)
1. **Comment Overload:** Popular YouTube videos and product reviews receive 5,000 to 50,000+ comments. No creator or brand marketing team can manually read and analyze them all.
2. **Superficial Metrics:** Views and like counts only show *volume*, but do not reveal *real audience sentiment*, emotion, or friction points (e.g., loud audio, slow pacing, high pricing).
3. **Missed Revenue & Strategy:** Creators struggle to price their brand deals, write high-CTR titles, or reply to thousands of viewer questions.

---

## 💡 3. The Solution: TubePulse AI
TubePulse is an end-to-end intelligence hub that automatically ingests YouTube video links or custom datasets, runs a multi-layered NLP and emotion extraction pipeline, and delivers:
* **Context-Aware Sentiment & 6-Axis Emotion Radar**
* **Aspect-Based Sentiment Matrix (ABSA)**
* **Real-time AI Copilot Agent (PulseAgent)**
* **Podcast-Style AI Voice Audio Briefing**
* **Creator Brand Deal Sponsor Valuation (₹ / $)**
* **Head-to-Head Video Battle Arena & Channel Competitor Spy**

## 🎯 7. Viva & Interview Q&A Preparation (Tough Questions & Answers)

**Q1: How does your sentiment analysis handle Hinglish or Internet Slang?**
> *Answer:* *"We built a custom weighted contextual lexicon in `lib/nlpEngine.ts` that includes Indian colloquial terms like 'paisa wasool', 'bawaal', 'bakwas', as well as Gen-Z internet slang like 'fire', 'goat', 'mid', and 'banger'. It also features negation handling (e.g., 'not good') and emoji weighting."*

**Q2: What is ABSA (Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis) and why is it useful?**
> *Answer:* *"Standard sentiment analysis only gives a single overall score. ABSA divides the product or video into specific dimensions (Audio, Visuals, Explanation, Acting, Value) so creators know exactly which component caused viewer satisfaction or friction."*

**Q3: How does the AI Voice Briefing work without paid external APIs?**
> *Answer:* *"We use the native browser Web Speech Synthesis API combined with an intelligent script synthesis engine in `lib/voiceBriefingEngine.ts`. This provides zero-latency, free, and natural audio playback with customizable speeds and visual soundwave animations."*

**Q4: Is the platform scalable?**
> *Answer:* *"Yes. Because the backend is built with Next.js Serverless Functions deployed on Vercel's global Edge CDN, each request auto-scales independently with high concurrency and low latency."*
